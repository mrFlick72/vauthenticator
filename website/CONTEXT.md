# VAuthenticator Project Website

The public project website introduces VAuthenticator to people considering using it in their applications. Its initial audience is prospective adopters; contributor content is a topic for later discussion.

## Language

**Prospective adopter**:
A developer or team considering VAuthenticator for authentication and authorization in their applications.
_Avoid_: User (ambiguous with a person signing in through VAuthenticator)

**Contributor**:
A person who helps develop or maintain the VAuthenticator project or its documentation.
